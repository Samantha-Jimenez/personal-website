### March has always felt like a new beginning to me.

Technically the calendar says the year begins in January, but something about <span className="font-medium">March</span>, the beginning of spring, feels like a <LeadIn>rebirth</LeadIn>. The earth is defrosting. Plants return after months underground. The daylight lingers a little longer in the evenings. The cold starts to loosen its grip. And new ideas begin to take shape.

This month feels especially meaningful because it is also my <span className="font-medium">birth month</span>. As an <span className="font-medium">Aries</span>, the zodiac marks this season as the start of a new cycle. A spark of forward motion.

I can feel that energy right now.

Over the past few months, my life has shifted in ways I did not fully expect. I moved back to New York after spending several years away. I packed up my life, said goodbye to Miami and my time as a digital nomad, and returned home. Now I am settling into Astoria, Queens, one of the most vibrant and creative neighborhoods in the city.

Being back in New York feels grounding. The energy here feels familiar. Fast, alive, and full of possibility. Every walk outside reminds me that <span className="font-medium">this city is a place where people come to build things, to create</span>.

#### Lately I have been feeling the urge to build and create again too.

Over the past few weeks I have been redesigning my personal website and preparing to relaunch this blog. The process has been both technical and personal, and somewhere in the middle of it, I started thinking more clearly about <span className="font-medium">what I actually want this space to be</span>.

Part of that reflection brought me back to something I’ve been working through for a while now.

Over the past few years, I found myself pulling back more than I used to. I kept a lot of ideas to myself and spent more time in my own head than out in the world sharing them. In some ways, I shrank a little, not intentionally, but gradually.

#### But deep down, I know that version of me isn’t my true nature.

<LeadIn>As a triple fire sign</LeadIn>, there's something in me that wants to burn bright and take up space. To express myself fully, without apology. Somewhere along the way I lost a bit of that, but I’m finding my way back to it.

In many ways, this blog is part of that process. A kind of <span className="italic font-medium">exposure therapy</span>. A way to work through the fear of being perceived and to reclaim the parts of myself that used to create freely without overthinking everything.

This space feels a bit like a digital diary. A place to get ideas out of my head and into the world instead of letting them bounce endlessly around my mind.

A place to tell stories.  
To document growth.  
To explore life through code, culture, travel, food, and whatever else I find meaningful along the way.

Instead of forcing myself into a niche, I want this space to feel open. Creative. Honest. A reflection of the many things that make up my life.

Coming back to writing feels like reopening a door that was never fully closed.

Not a dramatic reinvention. Not a sudden transformation. Just the quiet beginning of something new.

A fresh chapter. A return to building, sharing, and creating with intention.

#### And maybe, along the way, a reminder that I don’t have to be perfect to show up. I just have to be willing to be seen.

So if you are reading this, welcome back to the blog.
It feels good to begin again.
